RiftHunters

A co-op dungeon crawler where proximity voice, AI predators and physics-driven chaos create unpredictable runs.

Explore the depths of the Incontinuum with friends, solve puzzles, avoid enemies, collect loot and try not to die. Every run is different.

What makes it special

Core Features

Unique Class System

  • 4 classes with distinct passive bonuses
  • Each class can only appear once per team — strategic selection
  • Class-specific starting equipment
Hunter Cleric Scholar Thief

Emergent AI

  • Unique creatures with distinct behaviors — avoid their preying eyes and stay quiet
  • Light is key — in the dark of the dungeon, light is your best friend
  • Thrilling encounters — no generic monsters, just meaningful threats
  • Physics-driven chaos — ragdoll reactions turn deadly runs into hilarious moments

Voice-based Gameplay

  • Proximity voice chat as a game mechanic
  • Dead players hear the living, but can't talk to them
  • Use your voice to solve puzzles, scare enemies or lure them to your friends

Procedural Dungeons

  • Modular prefab system with connector-based generation
  • Deterministic seed sync for multiplayer
  • Every run has different layouts, traps, puzzles and challenges
